# Privacy Policy — The Hungry Bunny

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

The Hungry Bunny is a pure-veg restaurant in General Ganj, Kanpur. This policy explains what we collect when you use our ordering app or website, and why.

## What we collect
- **Sign-in details:** your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
- **Contact details:** your name and mobile number, so we and our delivery partner can reach you about your order.
- **Delivery addresses** you save, so you can reuse them on your next order.
- **Order history:** what you ordered, amounts, and the UPI reference number you enter after paying.

## What we do not collect
- We never see your UPI PIN, bank details or card details. You pay in your own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm etc.).
- We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Who sees your data
- Restaurant staff, to prepare and deliver your order.
- Our delivery partner (Porter), who receives your name, phone number and delivery address for that order only.
- Our service providers that store data and send sign-in emails, only to run the service.

## How long we keep it
We keep your account and order history while your account is active, and order records as required for accounts and tax. You can remove saved addresses at any time in the app (Account → Saved addresses).

## Your choices
You can update your name and mobile number in the app, remove saved addresses, and ask us to delete your account by contacting the restaurant. We will delete your personal details except order records we must keep by law.

## Contact
The Hungry Bunny, General Ganj, Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. Contact us through the support number shown in the app.
