# The Hungry Bunny

**Fresh · Local · Delights** — a pure-veg restaurant in General Ganj, Kanpur.

Order directly from us with The Hungry Bunny app: fries, popcorn, wraps, pasta, Maggi, chowmein, mini thalis, rice bowls, main course, parathas, poha, chai and coolers — delivered to your door.

## How ordering works
1. Sign in with your email or Google account.
2. Choose your dishes and delivery address.
3. Pay by UPI from any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and more) and enter the UPI reference.
4. The restaurant confirms your payment, prepares your food and sends it with our delivery partner. Track it live in the app.

## Good to know
- 100% vegetarian kitchen.
- No packaging charges.
- We deliver to selected PIN codes in Kanpur; the app tells you as you type your PIN code.
